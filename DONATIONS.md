# Donation and sponsorship

**GitHub Sponsors** is the public donation rail: recurring or one-time,
international, and built into the repository.

<https://github.com/sponsors/MasterShivnath007>

One-time Razorpay support is **temporarily paused** while the payment page is
sanitised. Do not share a Razorpay link until its public page no longer exposes
personal contact details or a registered business address.

Sponsorship is optional and does not change the MIT license. The public code
stays free, with no paywalled feature, telemetry, or mandatory account.

## What sponsorship supports

- macOS releases, dependency updates, notarization and release builds.
- Issue triage, documentation, and test hardware.

## What it does not buy

- It does not remove the MIT license or make the public code private.
- It does not add telemetry, ads, or a mandatory account.

If you need an invoice or a payment method other than GitHub Sponsors, open an
issue.
